

The house_price_prediction dataset was analyzed using different
visualization techniques.

1. Bar Chart:
   Used to compare the average price of different property types.

2. Line Chart:
   Used to observe how average house price changes with
   the number of bedrooms.

3. Pie Chart:
   Used to understand the distribution of furnishing status.

4. Histogram:
   Used to understand the distribution of house prices.

5. Scatter Plot:
   Used to analyze the relationship between property area
   and house price.

6. Correlation Heatmap:
   Used to identify relationships between numerical variables.

These visualizations make the dataset easier to understand
and help identify important patterns, trends, distributions,
and relationships in the property data.
