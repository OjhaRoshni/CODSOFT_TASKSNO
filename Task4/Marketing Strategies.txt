Marketing Strategies

1. Targeted Marketing:
   Focusing marketing campaigns on the age groups and customer segments contributing higher purchase amounts.

2. Location-Based Promotions:
   Providing targeted offers and campaigns in locations showing higher purchasing activity.

3. Category-Based Recommendations:
   Promoting frequently purchased and high-performing product categories through personalized recommendations.

4. Seasonal Campaigns:
   Increasing promotional activities during seasons with higher purchasing activity and introduce seasonal offers.

5. Personalized Offers:
   Using the customer characteristics such as age, gender, location, and previous purchasing behavior to provide more relevant offers.

6. Popular Product Promotion:
   Giving greater visibility to frequently purchased products through featured sections, recommendations, and promotional campaigns.

7. Customer Retention:
   Encouraging repeat purchases from high-value customer segments through loyalty rewards, discounts, and personalized offers.
