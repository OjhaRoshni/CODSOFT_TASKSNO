Customer Data Analysis Report

The customer data was analyzed to understand purchasing behavior and identify different customer segments based on age, gender, location, category, season, size, and colour.

Key Insights

1. Customer Demographics:
   Customers were segmented into different age groups to understand which age group contributes more to the total purchase amount.

2. Gender Analysis:
   Purchase behavior was compared between male and female customers using total purchase amount and customer count.

3. Location Analysis:
   Different locations were compared based on total purchase amount to identify locations with higher purchasing activity.

4. Category Analysis:
   Product categories were analyzed to identify categories generating higher purchase amounts.

5. Item Analysis:
   Individual items were compared based on the number of purchases to identify frequently purchased products.

6. Seasonal Behavior:
   Purchase amounts were analyzed across different seasons to identify seasonal purchasing patterns.

7. Customer Value:
   Age group and gender were combined to identify customer segments contributing the highest total purchase amount.

8. Product Preferences:
   Size and colour were analyzed to understand customer preferences and purchasing patterns.

Customer Segmentation

Customers can be segmented based on:
 Age group
 Gender
 Location
 Product category
 Seasonal purchasing behavior
 Purchase amount

This segmentation helps businesses understand different customer groups and their purchasing patterns.

Conclusion

The Power BI dashboard provides an interactive view of customer purchasing behavior. It helps identify important customer segments, popular products and categories, high-value locations, and seasonal purchasing patterns. The use of slicers for gender, location, season, and category also allows users to explore customer behavior interactively.
